Privacy Policy

Untangle Rope: Twisted knot · Stralen Digital LLC

Effective date: 24 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Stralen Digital LLC, a company registered in the United States ("we", "us" or "our"), handles information when you use Untangle Rope: Twisted knot (Android package com.untangleropetwistedknot.app) or visit its official website. Contact us at stralendigital@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.

1. Information stored on your device

The game does not require an account, sign-in, name, email or payment details to play. It stores your current level, completed levels, hint usage, ad interval counter, sound preference and privacy preferences locally on your device. These records help the game resume progression and remember your choices. The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise location access.

2. Advertising and consent

The Android game uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to deliver ads and manage applicable privacy choices. Ads support free gameplay:

  • One usable hint per level is free; additional hints are earned only after the rewarded ad reports a completed reward.
  • Standard interstitial ads may appear at the transition after every two newly completed levels; unavailable ads do not stop progression.
  • An adaptive banner may appear below the game controls when screen space and ad availability permit.

Google's advertising services may collect or receive IP address (which may indicate approximate location), device and app identifiers (including the Android advertising ID when available, app set ID and other SDK identifiers), device and app information, ad views, taps, interactions and diagnostic/performance information. Google may also associate data with its services where applicable. These data support ad delivery, measurement, analytics, security, fraud detection and, when permitted, ad personalization. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized, depending on settings, eligibility and applicable law. We do not require you to click ads or provide personal information to earn a hint.

UMP checks consent information and presents applicable privacy messages. Open the game's Help → Privacy & legal → Advertising privacy options to revisit choices where available. You can also manage or delete your advertising ID and related preferences through Android settings. If ads are not available, additional rewarded hints may be unavailable, but normal play remains accessible.

For Google's handling of data, see Google Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and My Ad Center.

3. Optional gameplay analytics (Firebase / Google Analytics)

We use Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase / GA4) to understand puzzle difficulty, improve gameplay and assess app performance. Collection is disabled by default in the app and is enabled only if you allow optional gameplay analytics. When enabled, the SDK may process app instance or installation identifiers, device and app details, usage and session information, approximate location derived from IP, and diagnostics. Our gameplay events include level start, completion, restart, duration, hint type and rewarded ad completion, and interstitial display. We do not deliberately include names, email addresses or other direct personal details in these gameplay events.

To change your choice, open Help → Privacy & legal → Optional gameplay analytics. Disabling it stops future optional analytics collection; it does not automatically erase information previously processed. Advertising-related measurement by AdMob is separate and is governed by advertising privacy options. See Firebase privacy and security and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Website and support

The official informational website does not contain our app's advertising SDK, web analytics tags, account forms or tracking scripts. The hosting provider may process ordinary connection and security logs such as IP address, request details and browser information to deliver and protect the site. If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any details or attachments you choose to provide; we use them to respond, troubleshoot, manage requests and keep necessary records. Please do not send passwords, payment details or sensitive information.

Where applicable, we rely on consent for optional analytics and personalized advertising, and on other lawful grounds such as performing services you request, legitimate interests in operating and protecting the game and website, and compliance with legal duties. Information may be processed by Google and its advertising or service partners, hosting and email providers, and authorities or professional advisers where necessary and lawful. Advertising-related data may be used for cross-app advertising where permitted. We do not sell user data for money; some advertising disclosures may constitute sharing or targeted advertising under applicable privacy laws. You can exercise applicable choices using the in-app options or contacting us.

We and our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Applicable transfer protections are used where required. SDK providers have their own privacy practices and may act as independent controllers or processors depending on the service.

6. Retention, security and deletion

Local progress and preferences remain until cleared through Android app storage settings or the app is uninstalled (subject to any device-level backup behavior). Clearing app data resets progress. We retain support communications as needed to resolve the issue, maintain necessary business records and satisfy legal obligations, then delete or de-identify them as appropriate. Analytics retention depends on our configured GA4 property settings; advertising and consent data are subject to Google's applicable retention practices. We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted network connections for SDK services where supported, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection or withdrawal of consent, where applicable, by emailing stralendigital@gmail.com. Explain which app and information your request concerns. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify or locate the data. Some SDK data is not directly identifiable by us; we will explain limitations and applicable alternatives. We may retain data where legally required. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. There are no game accounts to delete; clearing app storage deletes locally stored progress.

7. Children and audience

The game is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action. Advertising and consent settings must comply with the app's declared audience and applicable age-related requirements.
